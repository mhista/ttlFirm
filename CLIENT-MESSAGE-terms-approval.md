# Message to send to the client

Two versions. The first is the one to send. The second is a shorter variant if
you'd rather keep it to a few lines on WhatsApp and talk it through on the call.

---

## Version A — email

**Subject:** Privacy Policy & Terms are live — one section needs your sign-off before we submit

Hi Turuchi,

The Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions pages are now built and live on the
site, linked in the footer of every page. Before we submit the A2P registration
with the Retainer Engine team, there's one thing I need you to look at.

**What your document covered.** The document you sent had two parts: the Privacy
Policy, and the SMS Terms of Service. I've used both essentially word for word,
because that language is yours and it's already been through your review. The
Privacy Policy in particular covers every item on the compliance checklist —
information collected, how it's used, SMS message types, the "no mobile opt-in
or text message consent will be shared with third parties" statement, service
providers, opt-out via STOP and HELP, policy updates, and your contact details.
Nothing missing there.

**What wasn't in it.** The checklist also asks for **website terms** — the rules
governing use of the site itself — and an explicit statement that **contacting
the firm through the website does not create an attorney-client relationship.**
Your document didn't include those, understandably: it was written for the
texting programme, not for the website as a whole.

Because the registration can't go in without them, I've drafted those sections
so the pages are complete. They now sit as Part I of the Terms & Conditions page
and cover: acceptance of terms, no legal advice, no attorney-client
relationship, permitted use of the website, intellectual property, third-party
links, attorney advertising and prior results, disclaimers, limitation of
liability, and New Jersey governing law. There's also a short standalone
Disclaimer page carrying the attorney-advertising and prior-results notices.

**What I need from you.** I'm a developer, not a lawyer, and I'm not comfortable
putting language I wrote onto your firm's site under your name without you
reading it first. Please review Part I of the Terms page and the Disclaimer
page, and either approve them as they stand or send me your edits — or have them
looked at by whoever handles the firm's own compliance. Part II (the SMS terms)
and the whole Privacy Policy are your original wording, so those only need a
sanity check.

Two smaller things while you're reading:

1. **Dates.** Both pages currently show an effective and last-updated date of
   2 September 2026, taken from your document. Tell me if you'd rather they carry
   the date they actually go live.
2. **The consent checkbox.** Every form on the site now has an optional,
   unchecked SMS consent box next to the phone field, with the required
   disclosures and a link to the Privacy Policy. When someone ticks it, the
   notification email you receive records the exact consent wording, the time,
   and where it came from. **Please keep those emails** — that mailbox is your
   evidence of consent if a carrier ever asks.

Once you've confirmed the Terms language, we're clear to book the call with the
Retainer Engine team, set up the chat widget, and submit the A2P application
together.

Best,
Innocent
